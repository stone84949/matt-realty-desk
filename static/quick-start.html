Talk instead of type

Voice shortcuts

Click inside the box where you want the words first. After you stop, give the Dell about 5–8 seconds to type the result.

Super + H
Easiest: press once to start, speak, then press once to stop.
F9
Hold while speaking, then release. On compact keyboards, also hold Fn.
Super + Ctrl + X
Another start-or-stop dictation shortcut.
Super + Ctrl + M
Start or stop meeting transcription.
Watch for the microphone bar. It appears while recording, then changes to processing. A notification shows the words when transcription finishes.

Bring people in

Import contacts safely

iPhone: Contacts → Lists → press and hold a list → Export. Move the `.vcf` file to the Dell.

Excel, Outlook, or Google: Export contacts as CSV.

In Realty Desk: Contacts → Import contacts → select the file → review the count.

Built-in protection: matching email addresses and phone numbers are skipped, and imported people are never subscribed automatically.
